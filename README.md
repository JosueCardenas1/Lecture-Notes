# Lecture Notes – Macroeconomics, Macro-Finance and Econometrics

Personal lecture notes written in LaTeX, with step-by-step derivations of the main results. They cover macroeconomic theory, continuous-time macro-finance, Bayesian inference, asymptotic theory and time series econometrics. Notes are written in Spanish.

## Contents

### Macroeconomics and Macro-Finance
| Notes | Topics | PDF |
|---|---|---|
| **Macroeconomic Theory** | Microfoundations, complete markets, Solow growth model, Ramsey–Cass–Koopmans model, derivation of the New Keynesian model | [PDF](macroeconomia/macroeconomia.pdf) |
| **Continuous-Time Macro-Finance** | Itô calculus, consumption–portfolio choice and the HJB equation, stochastic maximum principle, two-sector macro-finance model, Basak–Cuoco, Kiyotaki–Moore | [PDF](macrofinanzas/macrofinanzas.pdf) |

### Econometrics
| Notes | Topics | PDF |
|---|---|---|

| **Asymptotic Theory** | Modes of convergence, Markov, Jensen and Cauchy–Schwarz inequalities, convergence of MA(∞) sums, law of large numbers, continuous mapping theorem, Slutsky, central limit theorem, delta method | [PDF](teoria-asintotica/teoria_asintotica.pdf) |
| **Univariate Time Series** | Stationary processes, AR, MA, ARMA, Wold's theorem, non-stationarity, optimal forecasting | [PDF](series-de-tiempo/apuntes_arma.pdf) |
| **Multivariate Time Series** | VAR, Granger causality, SVAR and identification, impulse response functions, FEVD, state-space models and the Kalman filter | [PDF](series-de-tiempo/apuntes_var_kalman.pdf) |
